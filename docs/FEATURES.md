# Features

What Grindstone does, screen by screen, with the sources behind each figure. The [README](../README.md) gives the short version.

Version 0.1, the full scope, on four tabs under an icon bar (⚖️ Gather, ⚡ Fuel, 💧 Hydrate, 🔥 Burn). The app opens on Gather, or on the Profile and its welcome (restoring a copy) until a profile exists.

- **Every screen** wears a header: the logo, the name and the tagline, leading where the app opens, a person opening the Profile at the right, then today: the streak, its count in gold beside its longest run, on a small line just above four tiles, ⚖️ the body mass weighed, ⚡ the energy eaten, 💧 the millilitres drunk, 🔥 the energy burnt, each green when its streak tick holds and red when it does not, each opening its tab. The tiles and the streak read again after every change to the records and every move between screens.
- **Every table** puts its figures on the right, its names and dates on the left.
- **The streak:**
  - four ticks a day, each counted from its card's oldest version (the weigh-in from the goals, Fuel from the fuel targets, Hydrate from the water aim, Burn from the burn target; the profile's first day for a card never kept), a day before it leaving that tick out: a weigh-in with the waist taped, the hip staying in Profile with the neck (your rule); Fuel, meaning the day's energy inside its band, the macros only informing (your rule); Hydrate inside its band with four fifths of the drinks water at least; Burn, meaning the day's workouts reach its burn target, any workout before a first weigh-in prices them. A tracker without a log fails its tick; all four always count, from the first record;
  - each tick held to its gauge's band as shown, with no grace: what the gauges show green is what the streak counts;
  - a joker for a day one tick short between two full ones, and an unfinished today that never breaks it;
- **Each tab** runs the same way: "Pick the day" (today when the tab opens, shown as yyyy-mm-dd), the log form, its title on the left and, on its right, "open the grimoire 📚" on one line (but on Gather, which has no grimoire), where the day stands, its stats, then its history.
  - **Stats:** the tab's chart over 30 days by default, 90 or ∞ (Gather's body mass and lean mass on one axis, each with its goal dashed; Burn with its target as a green dashed line; round ticks over thin grid lines, the x axis dated from the oldest day to the newest, the current values and a legend), then the tab's figures over the same window, up to today, a line telling what they read ("Average over 6 logged days, today left out."); every window starts no earlier than its card's first version (the goals, the fuel targets, the water aim, the burn target; the profile's first day without one), as the streak counts them:
    - **Gather:** the pace, the weekly change of the body mass, lean mass, fat mass and fat ratio (the slope of the 7-day average across the window, read per week, from 8 weigh-ins in it, or at least 4 spread over a fortnight; a day's weight swings about 0.5 kg with water and salt, so a week's slope is mostly that swing), each figure, never its name, green towards the goal, red away, steady under 0.15% a week or when the figure shown rounds to nothing (the lean mass's goal being a floor: rising is green wherever it stands, falling plain above the floor and red once at or under it).
    - **Fuel:** the average over the window's logged days, today left out until it is over, the energy and each macro a day against the average of their bands, coloured by it, then their goals (the energy's, its Goal tick; a macro's, its target painted, its floor without one), with the days that stood in their bands.
    - **Hydrate:** the average over the window's logged days, today left out until it is over, the drinks a day against the average band, coloured by it, with the days that stood in theirs, then each day's share of water against four fifths, as the tick.
    - **Burn:** the sum-up over the window's finished days, today left out until it is over, as Fuel's and Hydrate's: its days and its active ones, then a day's burn against a day's target, both on average over every day of the window, green once it reaches it, with the days that reached theirs; then its three biggest burners, the most burnt first, with their times and their minutes, reps and kcal in all, a time's average beneath, a circuit's steps counted with their exercise.
  - **History:** the tab's days month by month back from today, today in gold, the units in the headers and each day's streak dot, "Load {month} {year}" reaching a month further back, the months without records skipped. Gather's read BM, FFM, FM and Fat %; Fuel's kcal, Prot, Fat, Fib, Carbs, then Qty (g), the grams and millilitres eaten (pieces left out); Hydrate's the drinks and their water share; Burn's the burn, duration and reps. A tap on a day opens it under its row, the others staying as they are, a second tap closing it: its four ticks when the streak counted it, then its details, one row each, in white: Gather's waist (for now); Fuel's foods and meals by name with their figures and quantity, a tap on a meal opening its description and ingredients in a block as in the grimoire; Hydrate's drinks with their volume; Burn's workouts and circuit runs newest first, a run summing its steps and a tap opening them in a block, then volitional and spontaneous (the time off the clock and the fidgeting) in grey. × in the Streak column removes an entry, a whole run for a circuit, the history, the band and the streak reading the records again.
  - **Previews:** what is about to be logged shows on the day before it is: the food chosen or typed, at its quantity or a portion, with the plate in progress (Fuel), the drink chosen at its volume or a serving (Hydrate), the workout chosen with the session in progress (Burn), as a dashed ghost on the gauge and a line, "With the food: 1096 kcal, 22 of them spent digesting it.", "With the drink: 4410 mL.", "With the workout: 485 kcal.". On Fuel the core prices the day as it would stand once they are eaten, so the band itself moves with their digestion, drawn dashed with its maintenance, and the macros' gaps are weighed against the goals of that day.
  - **Search or create:** every chooser starts on "Select me" and narrows from three letters typed, starting matches first, then those containing them, ignoring case and accents. Fuel's offers its meals (💪) before its foods (🥦), Burn's its circuits (🔄) before its exercises (💪), Hydrate's its water (💧) before its other drinks (☕), each group in alphabetical order. A name nothing matches turns the form into "New {name}", with the fields of a new entry and an optional description, and "Add and log" adds it to the catalog and logs it in one tap.
  - **The grimoire:** the tab's catalog on a page of its own, the tab's icon still lit: a card titled by the tab, "Add to the catalog" adding without logging (or changing an entry by adding it again under its name), then "Your grimoire", its lists with each entry's description under its name.
- **Gather:** mass and waist, filled with the day's record when there is one, the body mass marked required when there is not, and the day's weigh-in removed from the same card.
  - Where you stand: the body mass and the lean mass each on a line, its goal labelled under its tick ("Goal 70.0"), the band already told above it, each titled with where it stands (the body mass with its band, the lean mass with how far it is to reach its goal or above it), the goal ticked, the day in blue, the body mass green within 1% of its goal (where the pace says it stands at it), the lean mass green from its goal up, its goal being a floor; the goals in force, else WHO's recommendations (a body mass index of 21.7, Gallagher's healthy fat). The lean-mass goal is the goal mass less its goal fat.
- **Fuel:** intakes from the catalog, one portion when no quantity is typed, the energy and macros to be recorded previewed under the fields.
  - The plate: "Add to the plate" puts foods aside, each with its cooking ratio (the cooked mass over the raw one, 1 when left empty), the plate adding up what each brings and drawn as a ghost on the energy band and the macros. "Log the plate" logs one intake per ingredient, all or none; "Keep as a meal" cooks it, per 100 g cooked, then logs the portion eaten, the whole plate when left empty, nothing for 0. The plate waits while the app runs.
  - Where the day stands: what was eaten and where the band runs, coloured by its verdict, then the energy on a gauge, the minimum (25 kcal per kilo of lean mass for a man, 30 for a woman, plus the burn) in red, maintenance (the day's budget plus its burn, digestion priced on what is eaten: the band starts the day without either and rises with each intake as with each workout) as a gold tick labelled under it, what was eaten in blue, and the band in green, past days included. The Goal tick is what the goal body (the goals' body mass and the lean mass their fat leaves) would spend that day, its budget plus the day's burn, and the body mass against the goals' sets the band's mode, said above the gauge (your rule): Leaning more than 1% above the goal mass, the band from Alpert (never under the minimum), dashed up to the Goal tick, then solid to 2.5% above maintenance (all dashed when the goal body would spend more than that cap, the gauge stretching to show its tick); Bulking more than 1% under, solid from 5% under maintenance to 5% above, then dashed up to the Goal tick when it stands higher; Holding in between, solid 5% either side of maintenance, the Goal tick merged into Maintenance's. The dashed stretches count as in the band. The Alpert tick, in green, marks the largest deficit the fat store can feed, maintenance less what the whole fat mass can spare (Alpert's 290 kJ per kilo a day), stepping aside when it falls under the minimum. Its labels on two rows, a tick's label under it and an edge's ending at it, keeping to the gauge: EA25 or EA30 (red) and Goal (gold, but in Holding), then Alpert (green) and Maintenance (gold). The carbs' floor and ceiling follow the band. The deficit that wears the spontaneous activity down reads maintenance, not the band.
  - Under the gauge, the four macros: Min, Cur, Max, Goal, the gap from the goal (Cur − Goal) and the share of the energy eaten, with a total. The recommendation: protein from 0.83 g per kg of body mass (EFSA) to 2.2 g (the upper bound of Morton et al. 2018's break point, past which training builds no more lean mass); fat from 20% of the energy (what is eaten, never less than the band's floor) to 35% of the band's cap (EFSA); fibre from 25 g (EFSA), no maximum; carbs from 130 g (the brain's glucose, EU Regulation 1018/2013), no maximum, the energy's cap telling what is too much. A target painted in Profile per kg of body mass is the Goal, its bounds 20% either side of it within the recommendation (your rule): Min the higher of 80% of the Goal and the recommended floor, Max the lower of 120% of the Goal and the recommended cap, fibre and carbs keeping none. Profile refuses a target outside the recommendation on the day it is kept (your rule): protein from 0.83 to 2.2 g per kg, fat between 20% of the band's floor and 35% of its cap, fibre from 25 g and carbs from 130 g a day, what the day cannot weigh going unchecked. Without a target, the Goal is the recommended floor and the bounds the recommendation. Cur and the gap are coloured against Min and Max; the macros never hold the streak's tick.
  - The grimoire lists foods or meals, a click on a meal showing its description and ingredients.
- **Hydrate:** drinks in millilitres, one serving when none are typed.
  - Where the day stands: what was drunk and where the band runs, coloured by its verdict as Fuel's, then a scale from nothing to a tenth past the max, as Fuel's, labelled Opt. under its tick, then the day's water and other drinks in a table, the water's share green from four fifths, food bringing a fifth of the water taken in. The optimal is the water target kept in Profile, or the recommended one, plus a millilitre per kcal burnt; the min a fifth under it, the max a fifth above it (your rule).
  - The grimoire lists drinks, water or other.
- **Burn:** two pills, a workout or the day off the clock (sitting, standing, moving). The workout's chooser holds exercises and circuits alike: an exercise's blank fields log a minute, one rep, one set and no load; a circuit asks its sets alone. What either will burn on the day's body mass is previewed under the fields.
  - The session: "Add to the session" puts exercises aside as steps (minutes, reps, load), a circuit bringing its own, priced a set at the day's body mass, run as many sets as the session, drawn as a ghost on the burn band. "Log the session" logs a workout per step, all or none; "Keep as a circuit" crafts it, then runs it by the session's sets, one when left empty, none for 0. The session waits while the app runs.
  - Where the day stands: what the workouts burnt and how far it is to reach the target or past it, then the burn on a line from nothing to 200 kcal, stretched past the target, the burn or its ghost, the target ticked, green from it on.
  - The grimoire lists exercises or circuits, a click on a circuit showing its description and steps.
- **Profile:**
  - six cards, each read top to bottom: its title, a note on how to fill it, its fields, a note on what the recommendation rests on, its save button (Keep this profile, Engrave these goals, Paint these targets, Store this aim, Fire this ambition, Keep this usual day), then "Forget it 🔄", which follows the recommendation at once; no date is asked: a card saves on today, a second save the same day amending it; the first profile starts every card not yet kept on its day with the recommendation;
  - the rusty you behind the numbers (birthday, gender, height, neck, and the hip, open to a woman only, nothing to forget), goals (body mass and fat, a fat under 10% for a man or 17% for a woman refused, the tracker's floor), fuel targets (protein, fat, fibre and carbs in g per kg of body mass, an empty one aiming at its health floor), a water target a day before the burn, the day's burn target and the usual day off the clock;
  - a field shows only your own figure; empty, it shows the recommendation in grey: the goals' WHO normal body mass index and Gallagher's healthy fat, today's recommended floor of each macro per kg of body mass, whatever targets are painted, the water (2.5 L a day for a man and 2 L for a woman, so that its floor, a fifth under it, stands on EFSA's drinks, four fifths of its adequate intake of total water: your rule), the burn target's 75 kcal a day from 2026-11-15 (your pick; before it, WHO's 150 minutes a week of moderate activity priced on the body weighed, any workout holding the tick before a weigh-in), and a usual day of 8 hours sitting at most (Ekelund), 2 standing (Buckley) and 2500 steps, the most of basal activity (Tudor-Locke), the workouts logged counting the rest; a card saved empty follows the recommendation, and a figure typed alone in the goals or the usual day keeps the others' placeholders;
  - each confirmation recalls what the card held before ("Before: 68.0 kg and 12.0%."), so a slip can be typed back; then the disclaimer;
  - past a divider, the settings close the screen: the vault's copy, every record written as JSON to the app's Documents folder for `make export-android` to bring to the Mac over USB, and restored from a copy `make import-android` pushes back, once `RESTORE` is typed, all of it or nothing, a copy from an older app brought up to this schema and one from a newer app refused, and a danger zone that erases everything.
